Support

DotToday: Daily Tracker

Contact us

Questions, ideas, or something not working right — write to dottoday@yandex.ru, in English or Russian. You’ll get a reply from DotToday’s developer, Nikolaevskii Dmitrii. In the app, you can start an email from the Settings screen.

What happens to your email and how long we keep it — see the privacy policy.

If something isn’t working

First, update DotToday from the store and restart it. If that doesn’t help, write to us and include what you can:

  • your phone model and Android or iOS version;
  • the DotToday version — it’s at the bottom of the Settings screen;
  • what you did, what you expected to see and what happened;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if it helps.

The app doesn’t send crash reports, so we learn about problems mostly from your emails. Please don’t send personal data: details about your health, records from the app, phone numbers or addresses. Take a screenshot on a test topic, or cover topic names and notes.

Where your data is stored

On your phone — and in its backup in your Google account or iCloud, if backup is on. DotToday has no accounts and no server, so we can’t see your records or restore them on our side. More in the privacy policy.

Moving your data to a new phone

DotToday moves along with the system backup or with a direct transfer from your old phone. The system updates the backup on its own, usually while the phone is charging and on Wi-Fi, so before you switch, make a backup manually — so that it includes your latest marks.

Android to Android

  1. On your old phone, turn on Google backup and back up now — in your phone’s settings, usually under System or Google → Backup. Menu names depend on the manufacturer and Android version.
  2. When setting up the new phone, sign in to the same Google account and restore from the backup — or transfer your data directly from the old phone, by cable or wirelessly.
  3. If DotToday wasn’t installed automatically, install it from the same store you installed it from before: its data is restored from the backup during installation.

Phones without Google services don’t have a Google backup. Whether the manufacturer’s own transfer app moves DotToday’s data depends on the manufacturer.

iPhone to iPhone

  1. On your old iPhone, open Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Backup, turn it on and tap Back Up Now. Names may differ between iOS versions.
  2. When setting up the new iPhone, choose to restore from an iCloud backup, or transfer your data directly from the old iPhone. A backup on your computer works too.
  3. DotToday is installed along with your other apps, and its data is restored.

Between Android and iPhone

Android and iPhone backups aren’t compatible with each other, so DotToday records don’t move over when you switch between them. The app has no export.

Deleting your data

  • Individual marks and topics — delete them in the app. Deleting a topic also deletes all its marks and skips. Deleted items disappear from the backup the next time the system updates it.
  • Everything at once — delete the app: its data is removed from your phone along with it. On Android, you can also keep the app and clear DotToday’s data in your phone’s app settings.
  • A backup on Android — in the Google One or Google Drive app, under backups: delete this phone’s backup. You can’t remove only DotToday from it: deleting the backup also deletes other apps’ data, SMS and phone settings.
  • A backup in iCloud — Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Backups → your iPhone: turn off DotToday and choose Turn Off & Delete. Names may differ between iOS versions.
  • A backup on your computer — in Finder or iTunes, together with the backup of the whole iPhone.
  • Your emails with us and your address in the list of testers — write to dottoday@yandex.ru from the same address, and we’ll delete them.

A backup may remain even after you delete the app — it’s deleted separately, as described above.
