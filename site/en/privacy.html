Privacy policy

Who we are

DotToday is made by one person — Nikolaevskii Dmitrii, the developer of the app. Below, “we”. To contact us: dottoday@yandex.ru.

This policy explains how data is handled in the DotToday app for Android and iPhone (on iPad, the same as on iPhone), in emails with us, in app stores, in testing and on this website.

Your records in the app

Topics with their values and settings, marks with their times and notes, skipped days and app settings are stored on your phone, in the app’s private storage: other apps can’t access them. A copy of this data goes into the system backup, if backup is on — see the next section. There’s no sign-up and no accounts, and DotToday has no server of its own.

The app doesn’t send your records over the network — on Android it doesn’t even have permission to access the internet. We can’t see what you mark or write in your notes, and we can’t restore your records on our side.

You keep these records for yourself on your own device: we have no access to them. Anything reaches us only if you write to us yourself, leave a review in a store or take part in testing DotToday — see the sections below.

Backup and transfer

So that your records aren’t lost when you change phones, DotToday takes part in the system backup and data transfer:

  • on Android — Android Auto Backup: the backup is kept in your Google account; when setting up a new phone, you can also transfer data directly from the old one;
  • on iPhone — iCloud Backup, or a backup on your computer if you make one there; you can also transfer data directly from your old iPhone.

The backup is made by your phone’s system, not by the app, and only if backup is turned on in your settings. It is stored and protected under the rules of Google or Apple and your account settings; we have no access to it.

If you don’t want your records in a backup, turn backup off in your phone’s settings; in iCloud you can turn it off for DotToday alone.

How to delete your data

Individual marks and topics are deleted in the app itself; they disappear from the backup the next time the system updates it. To delete everything at once, delete the app: all its data is removed from your phone along with it.

A backup may remain in your account even after you delete the app. In iCloud, you can delete DotToday’s data from the backup on its own; on Android, only together with the backup of the whole phone. Step by step — on the support page. We’ll delete your emails with us if you ask — see “Your rights”.

What the app doesn’t have

DotToday doesn’t collect, share or sell any data: not your records, not information about your phone, not how you use the app. It has no:

  • ads;
  • analytics, counters or tracking, including across apps and websites;
  • crash reporting service;
  • accounts, sign-in or sign-up;
  • server for data to go to.

Permissions

DotToday doesn’t ask for any permissions: it doesn’t need access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files or notifications — and on Android, not even to the internet.

Tile shimmer — a soft highlight when you tilt the phone — uses the motion sensor. This doesn’t require a permission: the app reads the tilt only while it’s open and the effect is on, and the sensor data isn’t stored or sent anywhere. You can turn the effect off in the app’s settings.

App stores and reviews

DotToday is installed from the App Store, Google Play or RuStore. Downloads and updates are handled by the store under its own rules and privacy policy.

Stores show developers aggregated statistics — for example, how many times the app was installed and in which countries — and sometimes anonymous crash reports, if you’ve allowed your phone to share diagnostics with developers. This information contains neither your records nor anything that would let us tell who you are.

If you leave a review of DotToday in a store, we see it the same way everyone else does — with the name the store shows it under — and we may reply there. The store keeps the review; we only read it, reply to it and don’t copy it anywhere. You can edit or delete your review in the store itself.

Links from the app

DotToday’s settings have links: one opens DotToday’s page in the store so you can rate it, one opens your email app so you can write to us, and one opens this page in your browser. From there, the rules of the store, your email service or your browser apply; the app itself doesn’t send anything.

If you write to us

Writing to us is optional: the app works fully without it. We need email only to reply to your question.

Please don’t send personal data. Describing your question is enough for us to reply. Don’t write about your health and don’t send records from the app, phone numbers, addresses or document details. If you attach a screenshot, take it on a test topic or cover your topics and notes.

  • What we see. The address you write from, your name if it’s set in your mailbox, and the text of your message.
  • Why. Only to reply. There are no newsletters or ads; we don’t share or sell your emails to anyone, except where the law directly requires it.
  • Where they’re kept. In a mailbox on Yandex Mail (Yandex LLC), on servers in Russia. We don’t transfer your emails elsewhere.
  • How long we keep them. We delete the conversation no later than 30 days after the last email. If an email still contains personal data we don’t need to reply, we delete it right after replying.
  • How we protect them. The mailbox is protected with a strong password and two-step sign-in; only the developer has access to it.

If you test DotToday

We sometimes let testers try new versions of DotToday first: on Google Play through closed testing, on iPhone through TestFlight. Taking part is optional. The list of testers and the feedback on the test version are kept by Google and Apple in their developer consoles. We use them only to give you access to the version and fix bugs, and we remove your address from the list no later than 30 days after the test ends.

Deleting your emails

At any time you can ask whether we still have your emails and ask us to delete them or remove you from the list of testers sooner. Write to dottoday@yandex.ru from the address you wrote from or test with — that’s how we’ll know the emails are yours. We’ll reply within 10 working days and delete within 7. If you’re in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you also have rights under the GDPR, including to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

This website

This website is a few static pages. It doesn’t set cookies, doesn’t use counters, scripts or external fonts, and doesn’t store anything in your browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As GitHub states in its description of GitHub Pages, when pages are opened, the host logs the visitor’s IP address for security purposes. GitHub keeps these logs itself, under its own privacy statement; we have no access to them.

Children

DotToday doesn’t collect data about anyone, including children. If a child wrote to us or takes part in testing, and you’re their parent or legal guardian and want their data deleted, write to us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

The date of the current version is at the top of the page. When the policy changes, we update this page and the date; earlier versions are kept in the site’s public change history.

If DotToday starts collecting or sharing any data, we’ll update this policy before that version is released and mention it in the update notes in the store.

Contact

Questions about your data or this policy — write to dottoday@yandex.ru. Answers to common questions are on the support page.
